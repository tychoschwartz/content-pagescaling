# @pagescaling — 30-Day Client Acquisition Plan

The goal of these 30 days: **land your first 1–3 managed clients** by turning
the assets you already have (posts, case studies, DM playbook, teardown) into
daily reps. This is a numbers game *on top of* quality. The plan is built so
that if you just do the daily target, results are close to inevitable.

**Primary target:** 500 personalised DMs sent, ~50 conversations, 8–12
teardowns, 1–3 closes by day 30.

Track everything in a sheet: `handle · niche · followers · SB trend · stage ·
last touch · reply y/n · notes`.

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Days 1–3, do this ONCE before DMing)

Your profile has to close for you. Every prospect checks it before replying.

- [ ] Post the **4 case studies** to @pagescaling. Pin the causewerefemales
      one (29M views) to the top of your grid.
- [ ] Post 1–2 of the **5 brand posts** (the flagship carousel + the reframe
      single) so the feed isn't just results — it shows the system.
- [ ] Bio: one sharp line + "Free teardown → DM" CTA.
- [ ] Build a starter list of **50 prospects** (faceless pages, 10K–500K,
      inconsistent/flat on Social Blade). Drop them in the tracker.

**Done when:** profile looks like a real operator's, and you have 50 names to
work through.

---

## The daily rhythm (~60–75 min, repeat every weekday)

This is the whole engine. Do it even when you don't feel like it — *especially*
then.

1. **Warm up (15 min):** like/comment on 2–3 recent posts of today's targets
   before you DM them. Lifts deliverability + reply rate.
2. **New DMs (30 min):** send **20 personalised openers** (playbook §3, rotate
   A–E). No links, one question each.
3. **Follow-ups (15 min):** hit everyone due for msg #2 (+2d), #3 (+3d), #4
   (+5–7d) from the sequence.
4. **Replies + teardowns (as needed):** anyone warm → book/record the teardown
   same day if you can. Speed wins.
5. **Log it (5 min):** update the tracker. Every DM, every reply.

> **Daily target: 20 new DMs + all follow-ups due.** Miss a day → don't "catch
> up" with 40 spammy ones; just resume. Consistency is the product.

---

## Week 1 — Motion (Days 4–7)
**Focus: get reps in and get the machine moving. Don't optimise yet.**
- Daily: 20 DMs + follow-ups.
- Send your first teardowns the moment anyone bites — even if they're rough.
- End of week: ~80 DMs out, first handful of replies, 1–2 teardowns done.
- **Check-in (Fri):** Which opener got the most replies? Are DMs landing (not
  going to requests/ignored)? If reply rate < 5%, your openers aren't specific
  enough — get more personal in line 1.

## Week 2 — Refine (Days 8–14)
**Focus: double down on what's replying.**
- Daily: 20 DMs, now weighted toward your best-performing opener.
- Tighten your teardown into a repeatable 3–5 min format (3 wins + 1 lever +
  offer).
- Start second-touch follow-ups compounding — most replies land here.
- End of week: ~200 DMs total, ~15–20 conversations, 3–5 teardowns.
- **Check-in (Fri):** teardown → interested rate? If people watch but don't
  bite, your close is soft — end every teardown with a direct "want me to run
  this for you?"

## Week 3 — Convert (Days 15–21)
**Focus: turn conversations into closes.**
- Keep 20 DMs/day (never stop filling the top of the funnel).
- Prioritise warm threads: follow up teardown-watchers, handle objections
  (playbook §7), propose starting.
- Nail down your simple offer/price so you don't freeze when they ask.
- End of week: ~300 DMs total, first close(s) very possible here.
- **Check-in (Fri):** where do warm leads stall — price, trust, or timing?
  Fix that one thing.

## Week 4 — Compound (Days 22–30)
**Focus: close, deliver, and let results feed the next round.**
- Keep 20 DMs/day.
- Close the warm ones. Onboard clients fast (kickoff + audit — your own step 01).
- Screenshot early wins → they become your next case studies and proof snippets.
- End of 30 days: **~500 DMs, ~50 conversations, 8–12 teardowns, 1–3 clients.**
- **Check-in (Day 30):** full funnel review (below). Then run it again — month
  2 is easier because your proof stack is bigger.

## KPIs — watch these weekly

| Metric | Healthy target | If it's low… |
|---|---|---|
| DMs/day | 20 | It's a discipline problem, not a strategy one |
| Reply rate | 8–12% | Openers not specific/personal enough (line 1) |
| Reply → teardown | ~30% | You're pitching too early; lead with value |
| Teardown → interested | ~40% | Weak close; ask directly to run it |
| Interested → client | ~25–40% | Price/trust/timing objection to fix |

One weak link breaks the chain — find which stage leaks and fix *only* that.

---

## Rules that keep it working

- **20/day, every weekday. Non-negotiable.** This is the whole plan.
- Personalise line 1 every time. Never blast identical text.
- No links in message #1.
- Follow up — most money is in touch #2–3, not #1.
- Keep proof honest: pages you *manage* vs the page you *edit* (see playbook).
- Ramp DM volume gradually from a warm account so you don't get flagged.

---

## Day-30 review → month 2

Ask: How many DMs actually went out? Where did the funnel leak? Which opener +
which proof snippet closed? Then: keep the winners, cut the rest, raise the
daily target if you can handle the reply load, and add every new client win to
your case-study stack.
