# @pagescaling — Roadmap to €5k/month

Honest, concrete plan. No hype. The whole thing rests on one shift:
**stop preparing, start shipping.**

---

## The diagnosis — why you're stuck

1. **You've been prepping, not selling.** Assets built, prospects scouted — but
   the money action (DMs → teardowns → clients) hasn't started at volume. Prep
   feels like progress. It isn't.
2. **You're spreading capital buying accounts.** That's slow, capital-heavy, and
   risky. The management business needs ~zero capital. You're funding the slow
   engine while the fast one sits idle.

The cure is boring: 20 DMs a day, every weekday. That's the whole game.

---

## The fastest path to €5k: MANAGEMENT clients (recurring)

Two ways to make money in this business:
- **A. Manage pages for others** (done-for-you service, recurring fee) — fast,
  no capital, stacks month over month.
- **B. Own pages and monetize them** (buy → grow → monetize/flip) — slow,
  capital-heavy, risky.

**Path A is how you hit €5k fastest.** The math:

| Price / page / month | Clients for €5k |
|---|---|
| €400 | 13 |
| €500 | 10 |
| €750 | 7 |
| €1,000 | 5 |

Recurring means once you land them, they stack. Land ~1 client/week and you're
at €5k in ~2–3 months. It's front-loaded grind, then it compounds.

---

## Pricing (so you don't freeze when they ask)

- **Starter — €400–500/mo:** daily posting, strategy, trending audio, weekly
  Social Blade report.
- **Premium — €800–1,200/mo:** bigger pages / more scope / higher cadence /
  hooks tested at volume.
- **Anchor to value, not hours:** "one viral reel can add thousands of followers
  — that's worth far more than €500/mo."
- First-month intro rate to remove risk is fine — but don't undersell. You're
  selling growth, not cheap labor.

Pick your number NOW and write it down. Freezing on price kills deals.

---

## The numbers that get you there

- 20 DMs/day × 5 days = **100 DMs/week**
- ~10% reply → ~10 conversations
- ~30% of those → teardown → ~3 teardowns
- ~30% of teardowns → **~1 client/week**

→ ~8–10 weeks of consistency = **€5k/month recurring.** Better close rate = faster.

The funnel only works if the top stays full. Never stop sending.

---

## Your 400K account = your unfair advantage

Most people pitching page management don't run a big page themselves. You do.

1. **Verify it's LIVE** — apply the likes filter to your own page. If it's
   engaged, it's gold. If it's soft, know that before you lean on it.
2. **Use it as your storefront** — post your case studies there. It's proof and
   credibility working for you 24/7.
3. **Instant DM authority** — "I run a 400K page myself" beats any pitch.
4. **Monetize it directly** — your own offer, affiliate, or promo = extra revenue
   on top of the service.

---

## Buying accounts: slow down

- **Most accounts for sale are DEAD.** That's *why* they're sold. You've now seen
  it live — big number, 15–190 likes. Apply the likes filter before buying
  ANYTHING.
- Owning pages is a wealth play (monetize via your own offers / affiliate /
  shoutouts / flip) — but it's slower, riskier, and eats capital.
- **Do it AFTER the service pays your bills.** Right now your saved capital is
  better kept than spent on vanity followers.
- If you must buy: only LIVE, engaged, monetizable pages. One good page beats
  five dead ones. Never buy a page you couldn't grow or monetize on day one.

## The 90-day plan

**Weeks 1–2 — Motion**
- Turn @pagescaling into a storefront: post the 4 case studies, pin the strongest.
- Lock your price.
- Start 20 DMs/day to your HIGH-priority leads. Send your first teardowns.

**Weeks 3–6 — Convert**
- Double down on the opener that's replying most.
- Close your first 2–4 clients. Nail delivery (kickoff + audit → daily posting →
  weekly report).
- Every early win → screenshot it → it becomes your next case study.

**Weeks 7–12 — Scale**
- Keep 20 DMs/day (never stop filling the funnel).
- Reach 8–10 clients. Systematize delivery so you can hold quality at volume.
- **Target: €5k/month recurring by day 90.** Realistic with discipline.

**20 DMs/day, every weekday. Non-consumable, non-negotiable.**
