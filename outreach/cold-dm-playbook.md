# @pagescaling — Cold DM Playbook & Proof Snippets

Paste-ready outreach for Instagram DMs. Everything here is built on the real,
verifiable numbers from the case studies. Personalise the `[brackets]` every
time — copy-paste-blasting identical messages is the fastest way to get your
DMs flagged and your open-rate killed.

> ⚠️ Honesty guardrail — keep your proof accurate:
> - **Managed by you:** @causewerefemales (29M views/30d), @moneyciety (+11K/14d),
>   @multimillionaire_mind (390K, accelerating).
> - **You EDIT, don't manage:** @victorianpoetry (+442K/30d). In DMs say
>   *"a page I edit"* — never *"a page I grew/manage."* It's still great proof
>   of your eye; just frame it honestly.

## 1. Who to DM (your ideal target)

Best-fit prospects — DM these, skip the rest:

- **Faceless pages, ~10K–500K followers** in monetisable niches (motivation,
  business, pop culture, aesthetic/quotes, money).
- **Posting inconsistently** — gaps of days/weeks, or clearly not daily.
- **Flat or declining** on Social Blade, OR good older content that's stopped
  hitting.
- **Owner isn't full-time on it** — side project, another business in bio, or
  a personal-brand owner too busy to post.

Why: these owners already believe in the page, already have an audience, and
feel the pain of "I know this could be bigger but I don't have time." That's
the easiest yes.

**Skip:** tiny brand-new pages (no budget), already-daily well-run pages (no
pain), and anything you can't actually help.

---

## 2. The strategy (why this works)

5 rules the whole sequence follows:

1. **Lead with a specific observation, not a pitch.** Prove you actually looked
   at their page in the first line.
2. **No link, no price, no "I offer…" in message #1.** First message earns a
   reply — that's its only job.
3. **Give value before asking.** A free micro-teardown or one sharp idea.
4. **One soft question** per message so replying is effortless.
5. **3–4 touches, spaced out.** Most replies come on follow-up #2, not #1.

Keep messages short (2–4 lines). Match their energy. Sound like a human who
runs pages, not an agency bot.

---

## 3. Opening messages (message #1 — make it catchy)

Pick ONE, personalise the brackets. Rotate so you're not sending the same text.

**A — The specific-observation opener (default, highest reply rate)**
> yo [name] — your [topic, e.g. "rich kid energy"] reel did numbers but I
> noticed the page went quiet for [X days] after. you sitting on a goldmine
> that's just not posting daily 👀 want me to send a quick teardown?

**B — The proof-led opener (when they have flat growth)**
> hey — been watching [@page], the content's genuinely good but the growth
> curve's flat on Social Blade. I run pages in your niche and just took one to
> 29M views in a month. mind if I send you the 2 things I'd change first?

**C — The curiosity opener (short, punchy)**
> [name] this page has way more in it than it's showing. can I send you a
> 30-sec teardown? no pitch, just what I'd do.

**D — The compliment + gap opener**
> your [format, e.g. "celeb before-they-were-famous"] posts are elite. only
> thing holding the page back is consistency — it's not posting daily and the
> algo punishes that. want the fix?

**E — The peer opener (page owner to page owner)**
> not a fan account I promise 😅 — I edit/run faceless pages in this niche and
> yours keeps popping up. you monetising it yet or just growing?

Notes:
- Lowercase + casual out-performs formal in this niche.
- One emoji max. Zero links.
- End on a yes/no question they can answer in one tap.

---

## 4. Follow-up sequence (if no reply)

**Message #2 — +2 days · deliver value unprompted**
> no worries if you're busy — here's the teardown anyway:
> 1. you're posting [~2x/week]; daily is the single biggest lever
> 2. your hooks are buried — first line needs to stop the scroll
> 3. you're not riding trending audio while it's hot
> want me to show you what a week of this looks like on your page?

**Message #3 — +3 days · social proof + soft CTA**
> last thing and I'll leave you to it — this is the kind of result the system
> produces: [drop ONE proof snippet from section 6]. if you ever want it run
> for you, the first teardown's free. 🤝

**Message #4 — +5–7 days · the breakup (surprisingly effective)**
> all good if the timing's off — I'll stop here. if "post daily + actually
> track it" is ever the goal, you know where I am. keep building 🙌

Then stop. Move on. Revisit in 4–6 weeks if the page is still stalling.

---

## 5. When they reply "how much / what do you do?"

Don't dump a price. Book the call / teardown:
> depends on the page — that's why I do a free teardown first. I look at what's
> pulling, what's dead weight, and what a daily system would do for you, then
> we see if it's a fit. want me to run it? just need [X].

## 6. Proof snippets (paste-ready — real numbers)

Drop ONE, matched to the prospect's situation. Don't stack them; one strong
number beats five.

**Reach / views prospect:**
> One page I manage did 29M views in 30 days — 75% of it reaching people who
> don't even follow yet. That's the discovery engine daily posting builds.

**"Does this actually work?" / skeptic:**
> It's all public — I can show you the Social Blade. One page I run is
> accelerating right now, its steepest follower spike is literally this week.

**Slow / stalled page prospect:**
> Had a page sitting flat, turned it around to +11K followers in 14 days just
> by getting the posting + tracking consistent. Same playbook works here.

**Big-growth flex (use sparingly, it's high):**
> A page I edit put on +442K followers in 30 days (1.87M now, all on Social
> Blade). I bring that same eye to the pages I run day-to-day.

**Credibility one-liner (bio / first-reply):**
> I run a portfolio of faceless pages — public, tracked, verifiable growth.
> Not screenshots-in-a-highlight, actual Social Blade curves.

**The system, in one line:**
> Daily posts, trending audio caught early, several hooks tested per idea, and
> a weekly Social Blade report. You watch it grow, I run the engine.

## 7. Objection handling (quick)

- **"I don't have budget"** → "totally fair — the teardown's free either way,
  and it'll show you what's worth fixing whether we work together or not."
- **"I can post myself"** → "100% — the hard part isn't posting, it's posting
  *daily* while testing hooks and tracking what works. that's the part people
  drop. that's the part I don't."
- **"How do I know it works?"** → "everything's public. Social Blade, insights,
  screen share — I'll show you live."
- **"Send me info"** → "easier to just show you on your page — 5 min, I'll
  screen-record a teardown. what's the best niche angle you want to grow?"

---

## 8. Volume & safety (don't get flagged)

- Start slow: **~15–25 personalised DMs/day** from a warmed-up account. Ramp
  gradually.
- **Never** paste the same message twice in a row — vary wording.
- No links in the first message, ever.
- Engage (like/reply on their posts) before DMing when you can — it lifts
  deliverability and reply rate.
- Track replies in a simple sheet: handle · date · stage · reply y/n · notes.
