What's capping it

Growth without a floor under it.

1

Reach without resonance

0.4% engagement, ~62 likes and 0.19 comments on 15.7K followers. Healthy for your size is roughly 1.5–3%. New followers see one Reel and never interact again — so Instagram never promotes you to the next tier, and growth stays capped.

2

The grid has no single promise

A wholesome baby clip, a $47B CEO drama, a random work-POV meme, a billionaire-tax explainer — all on one page. The bio says "entrepreneur mindset," but the feed is generic viral bait. Mixed signals pull in a mixed crowd that doesn't stick or convert.

3

Borrowed clips, no original voice

The content is reposted movie and news footage with a caption on top. Great for cheap reach, but there's no face, no take, no reason to trust or remember the page. You can't build loyalty — or a brand — on other people's footage.

4

No conversion path

15,701 followers and the only CTA is "Follow & Level Up." No offer, no free resource, no email capture. The audience you're growing has nowhere to go — so the growth doesn't turn into anything you can bank.

5

Momentum is quietly cooling

Daily adds slid from +69 to +23 over the window. Aggregated-clip pages plateau fast once the algorithm has shown the same format around. Without fresh hooks and formats, the green days get smaller.
